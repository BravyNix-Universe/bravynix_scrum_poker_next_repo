Frequently asked questions

Is it free? expand_more

Yes, Bravynix Scrum Poker is completely free with no limits on rooms or participants.

What happens if the host leaves? expand_more

The room stays open and votes remain intact. If the host rejoins using their host link, they regain controls.

How many people can join? expand_more

Up to 50 people can join a single estimation room without performance degradation.

Why can't someone connect? expand_more

Bravynix connects peers directly. If a corporate VPN or restrictive office firewall blocks WebRTC connections, switching to a personal network or mobile hotspot usually solves it.
